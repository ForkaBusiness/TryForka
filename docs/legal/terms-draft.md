## Replace Section 8 with: 8. Forka Pro, Subscriptions and Payments

### 8.1 Free and Forka Pro

Forka has a free plan ("Free") and a paid subscription ("Forka Pro").

- **Free** includes dinner planning for your household, the grocery list, nutrition estimates, allergy filtering, cooking mode, built-in ingredient swaps and a few cooking-help answers each day. It also includes up to 20 recipes of your own (typed in or imported) and up to 20 smart imports.
- **Forka Pro** adds:
  - planning for breakfast, lunch, school lunches and snacks;
  - goals for each person;
  - meal-prep sessions and "cook once, eat twice";
  - no limit on recipes of your own or on imports;
  - more cooking help, subject to reasonable fair-use limits that protect the Service.

The features of each plan are described in the Service and may change. If we remove a Pro feature you are paying for, we will tell you in advance, and you may cancel. `[LAWYER REVIEW: whether to promise a pro-rata refund if a paid feature is removed mid-term]`

If you move from Forka Pro to Free, everything you saved stays in your account and keeps working. Only adding new items beyond Free's limits is restricted.

### 8.2 Prices

Forka Pro costs **$4.99 per month** or **$39.99 per year**, plus any applicable taxes. The price is shown in the Service before you subscribe. `[LAWYER REVIEW: sales tax collection. Stripe Tax can be enabled; confirm Forka LLC's obligations.]`

### 8.3 Free trial (yearly plan only)

The yearly plan may include a **14-day free trial**, once per account. A payment card is required to start the trial.

- **When the first charge happens:** unless you cancel before the trial ends, your card is charged $39.99 at the end of the trial, and every year after, until you cancel.
- **The reminder:** we email you about 3 days before the trial ends.
- **Cancelling during the trial:** you are not charged. Pro stays available until the trial ends.

The monthly plan has no free trial.

### 8.4 Automatic renewal

**Your subscription renews automatically** at the end of each billing period (each month, or each year) at the price then in effect, and your payment method is charged, until you cancel. You authorize Forka and its payment processor, Stripe, to charge your payment method for each renewal.

For yearly subscriptions, we will remind you by email before each renewal. `[LAWYER REVIEW: some states require an annual reminder for yearly plans, or a notice a set number of days before renewal; confirm the timing, see the checklist below. This reminder is not built yet: the app currently emails only before a trial ends.]`

### 8.5 Cancelling

You can cancel at any time, online, in a few taps:

- **Where:** in the app, under **Settings > Manage plan** (Stripe's secure subscription page), or by emailing forkabusiness@gmail.com.
- **When it takes effect:** at the end of the current billing period. You keep Pro until then, and you are not charged again.
- **What stays:** everything you saved stays in your account.

### 8.6 Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, we and Stripe will retry it for a period of time, and Pro stays available for 7 days.

- **If payment succeeds** during that time, nothing changes.
- **If it doesn't,** Pro is paused until you update your payment method.
- **If it never succeeds,** the subscription ends and your account moves to Free.

`[LAWYER REVIEW: confirm retry period wording matches the Stripe settings chosen at launch]`

### 8.7 Refunds

**Payments are non-refundable**, except where required by law or as stated here. We may, at our discretion, refund a payment, for example a renewal charged by mistake that you report promptly.

When a payment is refunded in full, the subscription ends and the account moves to Free straight away. If you are in a jurisdiction that grants statutory withdrawal or refund rights, those rights apply.

`[LAWYER REVIEW: refund policy is a business decision. Consider stating a window, e.g. "within 14 days of a renewal charge", to reduce card disputes. EU/UK consumers have a 14-day withdrawal right for digital services unless they expressly waive it when they start using the service; check whether Forka targets them.]`

### 8.8 Price changes

We may change Forka Pro's prices. A new price applies from your next renewal after we give you notice by email at least 30 days before it takes effect. You may cancel before then.

`[LAWYER REVIEW: some states require notice 7 to 30 days before a price change on auto-renewing plans; 30 days covers the common requirements]`

### 8.9 Founding families

Accounts created before Forka Pro launched ("founding families") receive Forka Pro **at no cost for the life of the account**, as a thank-you for being early.

- **When it ends:** founding status ends if the account is deleted.
- **It can't be moved:** it can't be transferred to another account.
- **It isn't a subscription:** nothing is ever charged for it.

We may change what Forka Pro includes over time, as described in 8.1; founding families receive Forka Pro as it is offered.

`[LAWYER REVIEW: "for life" is a strong promise. Consider adding "for as long as Forka offers the Service" and what happens if the Service or Forka Pro is discontinued.]`

### 8.10 Fair use

Some features rely on third-party processing with real per-use costs (imports, cooking help, meal-prep sessions). Daily limits apply on every plan to prevent abuse, and are shown in the Service when reached.
