Features

Everything Forka does for your family.

From planning the week to the last step of tonight's dinner.

The week, planned

A week of dinners that fits everyone at your table, ready in seconds. Choose how long weeknight dinners can take.

  • Lock nights you've decided, swap any dinner
  • Busy night: something faster in one tap
  • Plans around what's already in the fridge
  • Suggestions when you want something different

It learns

After dinner, one tap per person: loved it, it was fine, or didn't eat it. The plan leans toward what your family actually eats, and away from what comes back untouched.

  • Each person's likes and dislikes count
  • Favorites come back, not every week
  • Ratings and notes on any recipe

Safe for your table

Allergies are a hard filter: recipes containing them never appear anywhere, for anyone's allergy, not just the household's.

  • Nine common allergens and three diets built in
  • Ingredients to skip, or just show last
  • Imported recipes checked too

Groceries

One list for the whole week, amounts added together and sorted by aisle. Tick off as you shop; add your own items.

  • Each item shows which meals it's for
  • Changes to the plan update the list
  • US or metric units

Recipes from anywhere

Nearly 800 recipes to browse, or search by what you have. Add your own from almost anywhere.

  • Links to recipe sites
  • Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest and Facebook posts
  • Photos of cookbook pages and handwritten cards
  • Pasted text, or typed in yourself
  • Collections, favorites, print and PDF

Cooking mode

One step at a time, in large text, with that step's ingredients and timers that keep running.

  • Read aloud, step by step
  • Hands-free: wave to change steps. The camera stays on your device; nothing is recorded or sent.
  • Missing something? Swaps that work in that recipe
  • Nutrition per serving, for any number of servings

A look inside

Forka Pro

Free plans your family's dinners. Pro plans your family's whole week.

  • Every meal: breakfasts, lunches and snacks, and a packable school lunch for each child, on the same grocery list
  • A goal for each person: more protein, lighter, growing well, or a calorie or protein target for adults. Shared meals, sized portions, estimated daily totals.
  • Meal prep: the week as one session of about an hour, in the right order, with timers and storage notes
  • Cook once, eat twice: a double batch of a dinner that keeps, eaten again a day or two later
  • No limits on recipes of your own and imports
