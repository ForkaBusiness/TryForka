FAQ

Questions, answered.

Getting started

How do I start?

Sign up with your email address. Tell Forka about your household: your diet, any allergies, ingredients you skip and who's at the table. Your first week of dinners is planned straight away.

Is there an app for iPhone or Android?

Forka runs in your phone's browser, so there's nothing to download. To keep it on your home screen like an app: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; in Chrome, open the menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Is Forka free?

Yes. Everything in Forka is free to use right now.

Is Forka free?

Planning your family's dinners is free: the week's plan that learns, allergy filtering, the grocery list, nutrition, cooking mode with hands-free, ingredient swaps, and up to 20 recipes of your own and 20 imports. Forka Pro plans the whole week: every meal, a goal for each person and meal prep. See plans and prices.

Planning

How does Forka choose our dinners?

It starts from the recipes that fit everyone at your table: no allergens, your diet, nothing you've asked it to skip. From those it picks a week with variety, within the time you have on weeknights, leaning toward what your family has enjoyed and away from what they left. Each after-dinner check-in teaches it a little more.

Can I change the plan?

Any time. Swap a dinner for another suggestion, swap a busy night for something faster, move dinners between days, lock the nights you've decided, or add any recipe yourself. Locked nights stay put when the plan updates.

How far ahead does it plan?

A week at a time. Plan a new week whenever you like; the grocery list follows the plan.

Which allergies and diets does it handle?

The nine common allergens: peanut, tree nut, dairy, egg, gluten, soy, sesame, fish and shellfish, plus any other ingredient you name. Diets: vegetarian, vegan and pescatarian. Each person can have their own allergies, and a recipe with any of them never appears for the household. Forka filters recipes, but it can't see what's in a packet: always check ingredient labels yourself.

Recipes and cooking

How many recipes are there?

Nearly 800 to browse and plan from, plus every recipe you add yourself.

Can I add my own recipes?

Yes: paste a link to a recipe site, share an Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest or Facebook post, take a photo of a cookbook page or a handwritten card, paste the text, or type it in. Forka keeps the source and checks it against your family's allergies.

Does cooking mode keep my screen on?

Yes, in browsers that allow it. Cooking mode also shows one step at a time in large text, keeps timers running, and can read each step aloud.

How does hands-free work, and is the camera private?

Turn on hands-free and wave to move between steps. The camera only looks for your hand, using a model that runs on your phone. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and the camera turns off when you leave cooking mode.

Privacy and your account

Is my family's information private?

Your family's details, allergies and history are only visible in your account. We don't sell them or use them for advertising. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, in Settings, under Delete account. It removes your account and everything in it, including your family's details and your photos.

Forka Pro

How much does Pro cost?

$4.99 a month, or $39.99 a year with a 14-day free trial. Prices in US dollars; taxes may apply.

How do I cancel?

In the app, go to Settings, then Manage plan. It takes a few taps. You keep Pro until the end of what you paid for, and you aren't charged again.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes: a full refund within 14 days of any payment, no questions asked. In the app, go to Settings, then Refund my last payment.

Will I be charged when the trial ends?

Only if you don't cancel. We email you 3 days before the trial ends, and cancelling before then costs nothing. Yearly plans also get an email before each renewal.

I joined before Pro launched. What changes?

Nothing to do: families who joined before Pro launched have Pro for life, free.
