What Forka does

The weeknight dinner question, answered.

A plan that learns your family

Add the people at your table, with their allergies, likes and dislikes. Forka plans a week of dinners that fit all of them. After dinner, one tap per person says who ate it, and next week leans toward what they did.

  • Lock the nights you've already decided, swap any dinner in a tap
  • A busy night? Swap for something faster that uses what's on your list
  • Tell it what's in the fridge and it plans around it

Allergies are a hard line

Recipes with an ingredient anyone at your table is allergic to never show up, in the plan, in search or in suggestions. Each person's allergies count, not just the household's.

  • Peanut, tree nut, dairy, egg, gluten, soy, sesame, fish and shellfish
  • Diets too: vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian
  • Any other ingredient you name: never shown, or just shown last

Always check ingredient labels yourself. Forka filters recipes; it can't see what's in the packet.

Groceries built around the week

One list for every meal you've planned, amounts added up and sorted by aisle. Tick things off in the store and add what isn't in a recipe. Change the plan and the list follows.

  • Two onions for Monday and one for Thursday becomes three onions
  • Each item shows which meals it's for
  • Nutrition for every recipe, per serving

Your recipes, from anywhere

Bring in the recipes your family already loves. Forka reads them and keeps them with the rest, checked against your family's allergies like everything else.

  • A link to any recipe site
  • An Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest or Facebook post
  • A photo of a cookbook page or a handwritten card
  • Text you paste, or a recipe you type in

Cooking, one calm step at a time

Cooking mode shows one step at a time in large text, with the ingredients for that step and timers that keep running. It can read each step aloud. Hands messy? Turn on hands-free and wave to move between steps.

  • Missing an ingredient? Swaps that work in that recipe
  • The screen stays on while you cook
  • Print or save any recipe as a PDF

Hands-free stays on your phone. The camera only looks for your hand, on the device itself. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.
