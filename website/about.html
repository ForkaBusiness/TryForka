Our story

Real food. Real families. Real simple.

Forka was born out of one simple frustration: figuring out what's for dinner shouldn't be the hardest part of your day.

We built Forka because we lived it. Juggling sports, school and packed family schedules, we watched the same question come up every single night: what's for dinner? So we built the planner we wanted: one that knows who's at the table, keeps everyone's allergies out of the plan, writes the grocery list, and stays calm and clear right up to the last step of cooking.

Forka is built by a small team, including two co-founders, with one aim: to take the stress out of the week's meals.
